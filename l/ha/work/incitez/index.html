incitez: ingantaccen ma'aunin ambaton
, an tabbatar da shi — kuma cikin sauri.

eyecite shine ma'aunin gaskiya don cire ambaton doka (yana ba da ikon CourtListener da Caselaw Access Project). incitez ya dace da shi — an tabbatar da shi — yana gudana ~31× cikin sauri a kan cikakken taƙaitaccen bayani (~64× don gudu ɗaya na yau da kullun), kuma yana rarraba jam'iyyu daidai inda tsarin takarda ya dogara. Takaitawa shine ma'ana: daidaito farko, sannan sauri, sannan wuraren da za mu iya yin mafi kyau da gaskiya.

Daidaiton da aka tabbatar
324 = 324

Daidaiton ambato-da-ambato tare da eyecite akan ainihin taƙaitaccen roƙo, wanda aka goyi bayan ta hanyar 215/215 ƙofar bambanci a cikin CI. Ba “yana kama da kusa” ba — an tabbatar da shi ta hanyar inji. Yarjejeniyar ambato ta kasance a 2,160 vs 2,159 a cikin babban fayil na 1.7 MB — ba a sayi saurin ba da ƙarancin tunawa.

Sauri
~64× cikin sauri

Gudu ɗaya na yau da kullun na taƙaitaccen bayani na roko mai shafi ~90: incitez ~30 ms vs eyecite yana sake biyan ~170 ms na Python/fara samfuri kowane lokaci. Mai dumi da inji kawai yana ~31× (31.6 ms vs 987 ms); gefen yana girma tare da girman taƙaitaccen bayani da yawan ambato.

Rarraba jam'iyya
183 vs 0

A kan ainihin taƙaitaccen bayani na roko a cikin demo na mai bincike: 350 ambaton, 183 jam'iyyu da aka rarraba a fili, idan aka kwatanta da ambaton 324 na cirewa mara kyau da jam'iyyu 0.

Tsarin gaskiya

incitez baya da'awar cewa ya fi ma'auni a ko'ina — yana da'awar cewa ya dace da shi, a fili, sannan kuma ya yi mafi kyau kawai inda zai iya da gaskiya.

  • Rubutun PDF na asali: ambaton ɗaya eyecite ya samo — ~31× cikin sauri a kan cikakken taƙaitaccen bayani, ~64× don gudu ɗaya.
  • Inda tsari ya dogara (misali TEBURIN HUKUMOMI tare da layin jam'iyya mai tsabta): incitez yana rarraba jam'iyyar daidai — “Albritton” a matsayin mai shigar da ƙara — inda cirewa mara kyau ya haɗa kanun labarai cikin suna (“TEBURIN HUKUMOMI … Albritton”).
  • Inda tsari bai dogara ba: incitez yana zama a daidaito maimakon zato. Ana bayar da wucewa ne kawai inda ya dace.

Yadda aka tabbatar da shi

Kowane gini yana gudanar da incitez da eyecite akan jiki ɗaya kuma yana kasa CI akan kowane bambanci — oracle mai bambanci, ba demo da aka zaɓa da hannu ba. MFIC ne — horo ɗaya Mecha ke amfani da shi a duk ayyukansa: bincike wanda marubucin ba zai iya wucewa a hankali tare da fitarwa mara kyau ba. Ga masu sauraro na shari'a, wannan shine bambanci tsakanin “amince da ni” da “ga ƙofar.”

A karkashin murfin

Tushen Zig mai tsabta (babu I/O) → iyakar C FFI → CLI na asali da kuma module na WebAssembly — injin guda a cikin terminal kai-da-kai da kuma a cikin demo na burauzar. Nahawun ambato da bayanan mai rahoto sun samo asali ne daga Free Law Project (reporters-db, courts-db, eyecite), ana amfani da su a karkashin sharuɗɗan BSD 2-Clause.
