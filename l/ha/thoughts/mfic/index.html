Amincewa da fitarwa ta AI sosai
har ya kai ga sanya sunanka a kai.

MFIC — Ikon Kai Tsaye Mai Sauƙin Karya horo ne don hakan: bincike mai zaman kansa a kowane mika mulki wanda ke samo hukuncinsa daga kwangila ko bayanan, ba daga maganar mai samarwa ba, kuma yana da ikon kin amincewa ko dakatarwa. Wannan shine tsarin sarrafa ciki a bayan Sarbanes-Oxley (COSO) — raba ayyuka, sarrafa rigakafi da gano laifuka, hanyoyin bincike, tabbaci mai ma'ana — tare da LLM (ko mutum mai sauri) a matsayin sabon bangaren da ba a amince da shi ba.

Don aikin da aka tsara, mai haɗari, wannan shine bambanci tsakanin “AI ta ce haka” da amsar da za ku iya karewa a karkashin bincike. Wannan shine horon da ke bayan duk abin da Mecha ke ginawa — kuma dalilin da yasa sakamakon tabbatarwa anan ya zo da kofa, ba alkawari ba.

Yadda za a amince da code ko bayanai da marubuci mai kuskure — LLM, ko mutum mai sauri — zai iya ɓata a hankali. Matakin ja na TDD yana ba da F kawai (gwaji zai iya faruwa); MFIC yana ƙara sauran ukun. Wannan shine tsarin sarrafa ciki na COSO / Sarbanes-Oxley, tare da LLM a matsayin sabon bangaren da ba a amince da shi ba.

Ma'ana: bincike mai zaman kansa a iyakar mika mulki wanda ke samo hukuncinsa daga kwangila ko bayanan — ba daga bayanin mai samarwa ba — kuma yana da ikon kin amincewa, dakatarwa, ko jagorantarwa.

Litmus: idan wakili guda ya rubuta duka binciken da abin da aka bincika, zai iya wucewa da aikin da ba daidai ba? Ee → mai iya wasa, ba MFIC ba. A'a → MFIC.

Kowace kalma tana da nauyi:

  • A Hanyar Injiniya — shari'o'in da inji ya bincika, ba a zaɓa da hannu ba. ¬M → kurakuran rashi.
  • Mai Sauƙin Karya — kowane hali ƙin yarda ne mai tsanani wanda ba za ku iya shirya shi a gaba ba. ¬F → gwaje-gwajen kore marasa amfani, ko oracles marasa ƙarfi ("baya faɗuwa" yana ɓoye fitarwa mara daidai).
  • Mai Zaman Kansa — tushen gaskiya mai zaman kansa daga mai samarwa (≡ raba ayyuka: mai yi ≠ mai bincike). ¬I → gwaje-gwajen haɗin gwiwa suna raba wuri guda makaho; axis ɗin da TDD ya bar a buɗe.
  • Ikon — tushe tare da ikon gazawa ko jagorantarwa. ¬C → telemetry wanda ke lura amma baya toshewa.

Tsarin mulki biyu:

  • Static (tabbatar da code, a cikin CI) — gajiya, bambanci, metamorphic, canjin shigarwa, tushen dukiya, ƙofofin aiki/leak. Mai gano laifuka.
  • Dynamic (tabbatar da yanayin da ke gudana, a cikin samarwa) — kwangilolin iyakar mataki, kiyaye tsarin/invariant, sake-kore zuwa madauki na baya. Rigakafi + gyara.

Nemi mafi ƙarfi, mafi arha da farko: yankin ƙarshe → cika shi; juyawa → zagaye-zagaye decode(encode(x))==x; nuni → bambanci; canjin-invariant → metamorphic; zai iya ɓata shigarwa mai kyau da aka sani → canjin shigarwa (lambar rufi, ba bool ba); in ba haka ba → tushen dukiya + raguwa. Fi son oracle wanda marubucin bai rubuta ba — oracle-free ya fi "darajar da ake tsammani" da aka rubuta wanda zai iya zama kuskure da kansa.

Canjin shigarwa, musamman: juya kowane bit/byte na shigarwa mai inganci da aka sani kuma tabbatar da cewa mai tabbatarwa yanzu ya ƙi shi; adadin da aka gano yana auna yadda yawancin tsarin yake iyakancewa — juya "Ina tabbatar da dukan tsarin" zuwa lamba wanda babu wanda zai iya yin mafarki. Kiyaye shi da gaskiya: haɗa shi da corpus na musamman (dukkan shigarwa masu inganci dole ne su wuce, in ba haka ba mai binciken da ke kin komai yana samun 100% ), kuma auna kawai yankunan dole ne a gano (padding / CRC-excluded bytes sune halattattun abubuwan da ba a damu da su ba).

Kafaffen kariya: tabbatarwa na yau da kullun yawanci yana gazawa I (wakili ɗaya yana bayyana tsarin kuma yana samarwa da shi) ko C (yana yin log kawai). Mai lura da gargadi kawai shine M-F-I amma ¬C — telemetry, ba kofa ba; bincike wanda ya gaza ginin shine cikakken MFIC. Shaida tana da mahimmanci — iko ba tare da hanya ba bai gudana ba; sanya tarihin ma'auninsa ta hanyar commit + hardware. Membobin ƙididdiga suna ba da tabbaci mai ma'ana, ba hujja ba — ma'aunin da aka sani, ba rashi ba.

Fasahar da ta gabata: canji, metamorphic, bambanci, gwajin tushen dukiya, allurar laifi (software V&V); raba ayyuka, sarrafa rigakafi/gano laifuka/gyara, hanyar bincike, gwajin sarrafawa, tabbaci mai ma'ana (COSO / Sarbanes-Oxley).

Wannan shine taƙaitaccen aikin. Cikakken bayanin fasaha — axis na 'yancin kai a zurfi, tsarin mai samarwa/mai amincewa, haɓakawa daga manufa zuwa ƙofofin toshewa zuwa sarrafa sirri, da misalai biyar da aka yi aiki da su — yana cikin cikakkiyar hanyar MFIC methodology.
