# Mecha LLC website

Present Mecha LLC's software and consulting services to prospective customers,
with clear product descriptions, recognizable app artwork, contact routes and
purchase policies. This is the public umbrella site at mecha.llc, published as
static HTML, CSS, JavaScript and assets through GitHub Pages.

The software catalog includes Mecha Validate, Mecha RotShield and Mecha BeamFlix.
Describe supported behavior accurately and distinguish working development
builds from products available for purchase or download. Product pricing,
release readiness and licensing require confirmed product decisions; adding a
catalog entry does not establish those terms.

Keep private operational details and credentials out of this public repository.
Preserve disabled checkout until its configuration and fulfillment requirements
are satisfied. Success means readable desktop/mobile pages, accessible product
names and icons, working links and assets, and passing site checks.
